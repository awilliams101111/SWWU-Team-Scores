# Specification of SWWU Team Format
Under proposal 1a and 2 of the SWWU team format vote of 2026. All archers will be assigned a 3 letter code of their `division` and `class` on entry. Division refers to the bowstyle being used: Recurve/Compound/Barebow/Longbow (R, C, B, L). Class is made up of `gender category` of Open/Female (O, F) and `experience` of Expereinced/Novice/Alumni (E, N, A). 

# Sighted Team
Higest scoring 3 recurve or compound archers from a single club including at least one female archer and a maximum of one compound archer.

# Unsighted Team
Higest scoring 3 barebow or longbow archers from a single club including at least one female archer.

# Novice Team
Highest scoring 3 recurve/barebow/longbow novice archers from a single club including at least one female archer.

# Notes
- Barebow/longbow archers are not allowed in sighted team
- Novice archers are allowed into sighted or unsighted team
- Alumni are not allowed in any team
- Teams are ranked first by points, with ties broken by the relevant ruleset for the round being shot. This varies by event.
- Incomplete teams are allowed. Should a club have no female archer qualifying for a given team then that team will be made up of only 2 archers from the Open category.
